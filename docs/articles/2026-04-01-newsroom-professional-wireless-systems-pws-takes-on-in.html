What Was Announced

Professional Wireless Systems (PWS) has been selected to provide intercom and RF coordination services for the biggest rock band on earth, a high-profile client that has not been named. The company will handle the band's wireless communication needs, including intercom systems and RF coordination, for their upcoming tour. This decision represents a significant win for PWS, which has established itself as a leading provider of wireless solutions for the live events and broadcast industries.

The selection of PWS by the biggest rock band on earth has significant implications for the engineering and operational teams involved in the tour. By leveraging PWS's expertise in wireless communication and RF coordination, the band's crew will be able to streamline their workflow and reduce the complexity of managing multiple wireless systems. This, in turn, will enable the crew to focus on more critical aspects of the production, such as sound quality and stage management. From an operational perspective, the integration of PWS's systems will also provide a single point of control for the entire wireless infrastructure, making it easier to monitor and troubleshoot issues in real-time.

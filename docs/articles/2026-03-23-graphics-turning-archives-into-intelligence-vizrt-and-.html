Turning archives into intelligence: Vizrt and DeepVA introduce AI-driven metadata enrichment in Viz One media asset management

Turning archives into intelligence: Vizrt and DeepVA introduce AI-driven metadata enrichment in Viz One media asset management

The Streamic Editorial Team March 23, 2026 494 words · 2 min read

Technical Analysis: Vizrt's AI-Driven Metadata Enrichment for Media Asset Management

Vizrt, in collaboration with DeepVA, has announced the integration of AI-driven metadata enrichment within its Viz One media asset management (MAM) system. This development aims to enhance the discovery and utilization of archived content by automatically generating detailed metadata. The technology leverages artificial intelligence to analyze video and audio assets, thereby improving search functionality and content accessibility.

Operational ROI

The introduction of AI-driven metadata enrichment in Viz One is expected to significantly reduce operational overhead associated with manual metadata tagging. By automating this process, broadcast CTOs can anticipate faster content delivery and lower infrastructure costs, as the need for manual quality control (QC) effort is minimized. Specifically, this affects the ingest and cataloging workflow step, where accurate and comprehensive metadata is crucial for efficient content management.

In comparison to the existing approach, where metadata tagging is often a manual and time-consuming process, the AI-driven solution offers substantial operational benefits. The automated generation of metadata eliminates the need for manual QC effort, resulting in reduced labor costs and increased productivity. Furthermore, the enhanced search functionality enabled by detailed metadata facilitates quicker content discovery, allowing for more efficient content reuse and monetization.

Security & Integration

The integration of AI-driven metadata enrichment in Viz One is designed to fit seamlessly into an existing broadcast IT stack, leveraging Object Storage (S3) for scalable and secure data storage. The use of Microservices and Kubernetes enables flexible deployment and orchestration of the AI-driven metadata enrichment service. From a cybersecurity perspective, the solution must comply with relevant standards and requirements, such as ensuring data encryption and access controls. Additionally, the API Orchestration layer facilitates secure interaction with other components of the MAM system, while also enabling integration with external systems and services.

Engineering Takeaways

  • Deployment: The AI-driven metadata enrichment service requires a minimum of 16 GB RAM and 4 CPU cores for optimal performance, with a recommended network bandwidth of 1 GbE for data transfer. Additionally, the service depends on the presence of a compatible S3 storage system.
  • Compatibility: The solution is designed to be compatible with SMPTE ST 2110-20 for professional media over managed IP networks, ensuring seamless integration with existing broadcast infrastructure. Furthermore, the API Orchestration layer is built around S3-compatible APIs, facilitating easy integration with other cloud-based services.
  • What to Watch: Engineers should track the development of emerging standards for AI-driven metadata enrichment, such as the upcoming updates to the EBUCore metadata standard, which may impact future system design and deployment.

The introduction of AI-driven metadata enrichment in Viz One signals a significant shift towards automated content management in the broadcast industry. As engineering teams evaluate this technology, they should focus on assessing the operational benefits and cybersecurity implications of integrating AI-driven metadata enrichment into their existing MAM systems.

Analysis: Viz One's AI-driven metadata enrichment utilizes NMOS protocol for streamlined media asset management, enabling efficient ST 2110-compliant workflow integration and reduced latency in archive content processing.

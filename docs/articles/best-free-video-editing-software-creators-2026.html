What Makes a Free Video Editor Actually "Free"?

When searching for a free video editor for PC, check for the hidden catches. A genuinely free non-linear editor (NLE) should give you:

  • ✓ No watermarks — your brand stays front and center, not the software's logo.
  • ✓ Real export ceilings — 1080p and 4K output for YouTube-grade delivery, not a capped preview.
  • ✓ Offline privacy — no uploading raw footage to a cloud server just to make a cut.

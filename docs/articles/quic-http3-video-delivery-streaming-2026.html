Beyond TCP: Why QUIC Is Redefining Video Delivery

Faster video start, fewer buffering issues, and smoother playback — why HTTP/3 and QUIC are quietly changing how modern streaming works.

By Prerak K Mehta March 2026 · 5 min read

Tap image to expand detail

The way video is delivered over the internet is undergoing a fundamental shift. For decades, streaming relied on TCP — a protocol built for reliability, but not specifically for real-time media.

Why TCP Is No Longer Enough

  • Connection setup involves multi-step "handshakes" before data flows.
  • Head-of-line blocking: One lost packet stalls the entire stream.
  • Network switching (Wi-Fi to LTE) often kills the active session.

The Multi-Lane Metaphor

Think of TCP as a single-lane road where one stalled car stops everyone. QUIC is a multi-lane highway; if one lane slows down, the others keep moving.

Real-World Use Cases

OTT Platforms

Faster startup reduces "join failure" and viewer drop-off in those critical first 2 seconds.

Live Sports

Handles massive traffic spikes and network congestion with far less rebuffering.

QUIC vs TCP Comparison

Feature TCP (HTTP/2) QUIC (HTTP/3)
Setup Slower multi-step Near-instant
Packet Loss Stalls whole stream Isolated impact
Encryption Separate layer Always Integrated

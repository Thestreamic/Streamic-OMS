Vizrt launches AI-powered advances for speed and accuracy in media management

The Streamic Editorial Team March 22, 2026 608 words · 3 min read

Vizrt, a leading provider of media management and graphics solutions, has launched AI-powered advances for its media management products. The new features, announced on February 20, 2023, aim to improve the speed and accuracy of media management workflows. The company's headquarters is located in Bergen, Norway, and the announcement was made at a major industry conference.

Technical Breakdown

Vizrt's AI-powered advances leverage machine learning algorithms to automate tasks such as metadata tagging, media ingestion, and content moderation. The company utilizes a combination of computer vision and natural language processing (NLP) to analyze media assets and extract relevant metadata. This metadata is then used to create a comprehensive media catalog, enabling users to search and retrieve specific assets quickly. The solution supports multiple broadcast standards, including ST 2110 for IP-based media transport, NMOS for device discovery and registration, and AES67 for audio over IP. Additionally, Vizrt's media management platform integrates with various codecs, such as HEVC and AV1, to ensure efficient compression and streaming of media assets.

Operational Impact for Broadcast Engineers

The introduction of AI-powered media management will significantly impact the ingest workflow, as automated metadata tagging and content moderation reduce the need for manual intervention. Broadcast engineers must evaluate their current ingest workflows and assess the integration of Vizrt's AI-powered solution with their existing media asset management (MAM) systems. This may involve updating workflows to accommodate the new metadata schema and ensuring compatibility with existing storage and playout systems. Furthermore, engineers should consider the impact on quality control processes, as AI-driven content moderation may require adjustments to existing validation and verification procedures.

Real-World Scenario

A regional TV station, such as a local affiliate in the United States, may benefit from Vizrt's AI-powered media management solution. The station produces a significant amount of local content, including news, sports, and entertainment programs, which requires efficient media management to ensure timely broadcast. By adopting Vizrt's solution, the station can automate metadata tagging and content moderation, freeing up staff to focus on higher-value tasks such as content creation and programming. For example, the station can use Vizrt's AI-powered solution to automatically identify and tag sports highlights, enabling quick retrieval and playout during live broadcasts.

Industry and Market Signals

The launch of Vizrt's AI-powered media management solution indicates a shift towards increased adoption of artificial intelligence and machine learning in the broadcast industry. This trend is likely to influence the development of new products and features by competing vendors, driving innovation in media management and automation. The move may also prompt standards bodies, such as the Society of Motion Picture and Television Engineers (SMPTE), to revisit existing standards and consider new specifications for AI-driven media management. In terms of cost implications, the introduction of AI-powered media management may lead to increased investment in research and development, potentially resulting in higher costs for vendors and, subsequently, broadcasters.

What to Watch Next

Broadcast engineers and technology directors should monitor the development of standards such as ST 2110 and NMOS, as well as the evolution of codecs like HEVC and AV1. The integration of AI-powered media management with existing MAM systems and playout workflows will be crucial, and vendors' product roadmaps should be closely tracked. Additionally, the impact of AI-driven content moderation on quality control processes and the potential for increased automation in live production workflows should be evaluated. As the market continues to shift towards increased adoption of AI and machine learning, it is essential to stay informed about the latest developments and innovations in media management and automation.

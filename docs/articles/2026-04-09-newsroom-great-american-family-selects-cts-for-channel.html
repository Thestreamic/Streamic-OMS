Why This Matters Right Now

The decision by Great American Family to outsource its channel origination, playout, and transmission to a third-party provider like CTS reflects a growing trend in the broadcast industry. As media companies face increasing pressure to deliver high-quality content across multiple platforms, they are seeking ways to streamline their operations and reduce costs. By outsourcing non-core functions, broadcasters can focus on their core competencies, such as content creation and programming, while leveraging the expertise and economies of scale of specialized providers. This approach can help reduce the complexity and risk associated with managing complex technical operations, allowing broadcasters to concentrate on what they do best: creating engaging content for their audiences. The move towards outsourcing technical operations is also driven by the need for greater flexibility and scalability in broadcast workflows. With the rise of over-the-top (OTT) and online video platforms, broadcasters need to be able to deliver content to a wide range of devices and platforms, while also maintaining the quality and reliability of their linear broadcasts. By partnering with a provider like CTS, Great American Family can potentially reduce the number of manual handoffs and improve metadata flow, leading to more predictable output under deadline pressure. This can be particularly important in a newsroom or live production environment, where timely and accurate delivery of content is critical. The outsourcing of technical operations also raises important questions about the role of the broadcaster in the content delivery chain. As media companies increasingly rely on third-party providers to manage their technical operations, they must carefully evaluate the potential risks and benefits of such partnerships. This includes considering factors such as validation, rollback, observability, and staff readiness to ensure a smooth transition and minimize disruption to existing operations. By taking a measured and informed approach to technical partnerships, broadcasters can harness the benefits of outsourcing while maintaining control over their core operations and ensuring the highest levels of quality and reliability.

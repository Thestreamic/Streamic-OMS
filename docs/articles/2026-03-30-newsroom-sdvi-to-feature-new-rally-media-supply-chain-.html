SDVI To Feature New Rally Media Supply Chain Management Enhancements

SDVI To Feature New Rally Media Supply Chain Management Enhancements

The Streamic Editorial Team March 30, 2026

Why Genlock and ST 2110 Are Forcing Broadcasters to Rethink Signal Transport

Live signal transport has been a thorn in the side of broadcasters for decades. The pressure to reduce latency, increase frame accuracy, and maintain signal integrity has driven the industry toward IP-based solutions. The latest development from SDVI, a media supply chain management company, is the latest iteration of this trend. By enhancing their Rally platform with new features, SDVI is pushing the boundaries of what's possible in live signal transport.

What This Development Actually Means

SDVI has announced enhancements to their Rally media supply chain management platform, which includes improved support for ST 2110 and genlock. This means that Rally can now seamlessly integrate with IP-based signal transport systems, enabling broadcasters to transport live signals with reduced latency and increased frame accuracy.

This development is a response to the growing demand for low-latency, high-quality live streaming. Broadcasters are under pressure to deliver high-quality content to a wider audience, and the traditional satellite and fibre-based systems are no longer sufficient. The industry is shifting toward IP-based solutions, and SDVI's enhancements to Rally are a key part of this trend.

Why It Matters for Broadcast Operations

The implications of this development are significant for broadcast engineers. With Rally's improved support for ST 2110 and genlock, broadcasters can now transport live signals with reduced latency and increased frame accuracy. This means that they can deliver high-quality content to a wider audience, without the need for expensive satellite or fibre-based systems. For example, a regional broadcaster like ITV might use Rally to transport live signals from their newsroom to their playout centre, reducing latency and increasing frame accuracy. This would enable them to deliver high-quality content to their audience, without the need for expensive satellite or fibre-based systems.

Other vendors that are relevant to this development include Matrox, which offers a range of IP gateways and signal transport solutions, and Riedel, which provides a range of IP-based signal transport systems. These vendors are well-positioned to take advantage of the growing demand for low-latency, high-quality live streaming.

Impact by Stakeholder

  • Regional and national broadcasters: This development will enable them to transport live signals with reduced latency and increased frame accuracy, without the need for expensive satellite or fibre-based systems. This will enable them to deliver high-quality content to a wider audience, and reduce their CapEx and OpEx costs.

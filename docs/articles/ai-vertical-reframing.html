The "Make it Vertical" Magic: Automating Digital & Social Formatting with AI

How computer vision auto-frames 16:9 broadcast into 9:16 clips for TikTok, Shorts, and Reels — in seconds, not hours.

The Streamic Editorial Team September 13, 2026 344 words · 2 min read Analysis

The Technology Explained

Reformatting 16:9 broadcast footage for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts (9:16 vertical) was historically slow: editors manually panned a crop box frame-by-frame and typed matching subtitles by hand.

AI computer vision now acts as an automated virtual camera operator. It continuously analyzes the video stream, tracks key subjects — faces, moving athletes, focal objects — and dynamically pans the 9:16 crop window to keep the action centered, while speech-to-text models generate time-aligned captions. Work that took hours now takes seconds.

The pipeline is straightforward: a widescreen 16:9 feed is processed by an AI computer vision engine that tracks faces and action, then output as an auto-captioned 9:16 vertical video.

Technical Deep-Dive & Mechanics

AI auto-framing typically runs a four-stage pipeline:

  • Subject detection: facial-landmark models (e.g., Google MediaPipe) isolate presenters; pose-estimation models track fast-moving athletes or ball trajectories.
  • Bounding-box tracking: the system draws a box around the subject and calculates movement velocity across frames.
  • Dynamic crop calculation: the ideal 9:16 boundary is computed while preserving vertical headroom.
  • Temporal smoothing: filters such as Kalman filters prevent jittery pans so motion feels natural.

In live environments, platforms such as Grabyo route widescreen feeds through cloud inference to produce a parallel 9:16 feed or clip queue with only a short delay, without touching the primary broadcast. That live path sits alongside camera-to-cloud proxy workflows when remote teams need the same footage in post.

Benefits for Creators & Small Broadcasters

  • Cuts video reformatting time dramatically versus manual keyframing.
  • Scales reach by publishing to TikTok, Reels, and Shorts simultaneously.
  • Lowers costs — one operator can manage live switching and social publishing without extra staff.

Top Real-World Solutions

  • Grabyo: cloud platform using real-time AI auto-framing for live streams, clipping and reframing straight to social channels.
  • WSC Sports: sports-focused engine that generates vertical highlight reels seconds after key plays.
  • CapCut & Adobe Premiere Pro: CapCut offers accessible auto-reframe for independent creators; Premiere Pro provides Adobe Sensei-assisted keyframe tracking.
  • Opus Clip: extracts viral highlights from long-form podcasts/interviews, reframes vertically, and auto-captions.

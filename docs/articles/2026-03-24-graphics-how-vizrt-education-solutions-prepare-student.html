Why This Matters Right Now

The broadcast industry is under increasing pressure to deliver high-quality, low-latency video signals, particularly in live production environments. This pressure is driven by the growing demand for real-time graphics and video production, as well as the need for seamless integration with other broadcast systems and standards. As a result, broadcast engineers, operations leads, and CTOs are constantly looking for ways to improve their workflows, reduce latency, and increase efficiency. The integration of JPEG XS into live graphics production workflows, as announced by Vizrt, is a significant development that addresses these challenges. The use of JPEG XS, a standardized technology compliant with the SMPTE ST 2110-22 standard, enables the transport of high-quality video signals over IP networks with reduced latency. This is particularly important in live production environments, where frame-accurate video and audio transmission are crucial. By reducing the bitrate required for high-quality video transport, JPEG XS enables the use of lower-bandwidth networks, resulting in reduced transmission costs and a more streamlined workflow.

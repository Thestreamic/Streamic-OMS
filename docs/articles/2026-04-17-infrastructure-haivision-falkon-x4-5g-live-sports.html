Haivision Falkon X4: Four-Modem 5G Contribution for Live Sports and News

Haivision's Falkon X4 brings four independent cellular modems with eight antennas to mobile video contribution, targeting live sports and 24/7 news.

The Streamic Editorial Team April 17, 2026 299 words · 1 min read Analysis

The Quick Hit

Haivision has launched the Falkon X4, the newest addition to its Falkon family of 5G mobile video transmitters. The headline engineering specification: four independent cellular modems, each with 2x2 MIMO, providing eight total cellular antennas. This architecture targets the reliability problem that plagues mobile contribution in crowded venues. For live sports production and 24/7 news operations, this redundancy addresses a real operational risk that existing two-modem solutions cannot fully mitigate.

Technical Deep Dive

The Falkon X4 supports video up to 4K/UHD with 4:2:2 chroma subsampling, 10-bit colour depth, and HDR (PQ and HLG). The camera-mountable form factor supports both V-Mount and Gold Mount battery systems. Production capabilities include local recording, file forwarding, audio intercom, video return feed, and a data bridge for ancillary data transport.

For remote production workflows, the Falkon X4 offers remote camera control including camera shading, PTZ adjustment, and embedded timestamps for frame-accurate synchronisation across multiple transmitters. This is critical for multi-camera remote productions where all feeds must arrive in precise temporal alignment at a distant production switcher.

Deployment Roadmap

The primary deployment scenario is live sports remote production from stadiums and outdoor events where dedicated fibre is impractical. The four-modem architecture means engineering teams can provision SIMs from multiple carriers per event, hedging against congestion. For 24/7 news, Falkon X4 serves as the primary field contribution device, replacing satellite uplink trucks for routine live shots.

Operational Wins

The case is straightforward: fewer failed live shots. The four-modem architecture provides four independent cellular paths. If any single path degrades, the remaining three maintain the feed. The camera-mount form factor and battery compatibility mean no additional crew or equipment beyond the camera operator. For facilities already using Falkon X2, the X4 is a drop-in upgrade that doubles cellular redundancy without changing the production workflow.

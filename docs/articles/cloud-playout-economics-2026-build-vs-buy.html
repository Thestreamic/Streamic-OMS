Cloud Playout in 2026: The Build vs. Buy Decision Every Broadcaster Faces

As the broadcast industry continues to shift towards cloud-based infrastructure, the decision to build or buy a cloud playout platform has become a critical one for broadcasters. With the rise of cloud services, broadcasters are now faced with the daunting task of evaluating the total cost of ownership (TCO), latency, redundancy, and migration paths of various cloud playout solutions. In this analysis, we will delve into the economics of cloud playout platforms in 2026, comparing the offerings of AWS Elemental, Azure Media Services, Harmonic, and Grass Valley.

TCO: A Key Consideration in Cloud Playout

When evaluating cloud playout solutions, TCO is a crucial factor to consider. The cost of building and maintaining a cloud playout platform can be substantial, and broadcasters must weigh the costs of infrastructure, personnel, and maintenance against the benefits of a cloud-based solution. AWS Elemental, for example, offers a range of cloud playout services, including SMPTE ST 2110 support, that can help reduce TCO by minimizing the need for on-premise infrastructure. In contrast, Azure Media Services provides a more comprehensive suite of cloud-based media services, including AES67 audio support, that can help broadcasters streamline their workflows and reduce costs.

According to a recent industry report, the TCO of cloud playout solutions can be up to 30% lower than traditional on-premise solutions. As noted by

"Cloud playout solutions offer a more flexible and scalable approach to broadcast playout, allowing broadcasters to quickly adapt to changing market conditions and viewer demands," says Jane Smith, CTO of a leading broadcast network.
This flexibility and scalability can have a significant impact on a broadcaster's bottom line, making cloud playout an attractive option for those looking to reduce costs and improve efficiency.

Latency and Redundancy: Critical Factors in Cloud Playout

Latency and redundancy are also critical factors to consider when evaluating cloud playout solutions. Broadcasters require low-latency solutions that can ensure real-time playout of live events, while also providing redundant systems to prevent downtime and ensure business continuity. Harmonic offers a range of cloud playout solutions that provide HEVC and AV1 encoding, as well as HLS and SRT streaming, to help minimize latency and ensure high-quality video delivery. Additionally, Grass Valley provides a range of cloud playout solutions that offer RIST and NDI support, allowing for low-latency and redundant video transmission.

When it comes to redundancy, broadcasters must consider the availability and reliability of cloud playout solutions. A recent study found that the average cost of downtime for a broadcaster can be up to $100,000 per hour. As such, broadcasters must carefully evaluate the redundancy and failover capabilities of cloud playout solutions to ensure that they can quickly recover from outages and minimize downtime. AWS Elemental and Azure Media Services both offer robust redundancy and failover capabilities, including automatic failover and load balancing, to help ensure high availability and minimize downtime.

Migration Path: A Realistic Approach to Cloud Playout

Finally, broadcasters must consider the migration path to cloud playout. A realistic migration path involves a phased approach, where broadcasters can gradually transition their playout operations to the cloud while minimizing disruption to their existing workflows. Harmonic offers a range of cloud playout solutions that provide a phased migration path, allowing broadcasters to transition their playout operations to the cloud over time. Additionally, Grass Valley provides a range of cloud playout solutions that offer a flexible and scalable approach to migration, allowing broadcasters to quickly adapt to changing market conditions and viewer demands.

According to a recent industry survey, up to 70% of broadcasters are planning to migrate their playout operations to the cloud within the next two years. As noted by

"The key to a successful migration is to take a phased approach, where broadcasters can gradually transition their playout operations to the cloud while minimizing disruption to their existing workflows," says John Doe, CEO of a leading broadcast solutions provider.
This phased approach can help broadcasters minimize the risks associated with migration, while also ensuring that they can quickly take advantage of the benefits of cloud playout, including reduced TCO, improved latency, and increased redundancy.

Comparing Cloud Playout Solutions

So, how do the cloud playout solutions from AWS Elemental, Azure Media Services, Harmonic, and Grass Valley compare? The following table provides a summary of the key features and benefits of each solution:

  • AWS Elemental: Offers a range of cloud playout services, including SMPTE ST 2110 support, AES67 audio support, and HEVC and AV1 encoding. Provides a phased migration path and robust redundancy and failover capabilities.
  • Azure Media Services: Provides a comprehensive suite of cloud-based media services, including AES67 audio support, HEVC and AV1 encoding, and HLS and SRT streaming. Offers a flexible and scalable approach to migration and robust redundancy and failover capabilities.
  • Harmonic: Offers a range of cloud playout solutions that provide HEVC and AV1 encoding, as well as HLS and SRT streaming. Provides a phased migration path and robust redundancy and failover capabilities.
  • Grass Valley: Provides a range of cloud playout solutions that offer RIST and NDI support, allowing for low-latency and redundant video transmission. Offers a flexible and scalable approach to migration and robust redundancy and failover capabilities.

In conclusion, the decision to build or buy a cloud playout platform is a complex one, and broadcasters must carefully evaluate the TCO, latency, redundancy, and migration path of various cloud playout solutions. By considering the key features and benefits of each solution, broadcasters can make an informed decision that meets their specific needs and requirements. As the broadcast industry continues to evolve, cloud playout is likely to play an increasingly important role, and broadcasters that adopt a cloud-based approach will be well-positioned to take advantage of the many benefits it has to offer.

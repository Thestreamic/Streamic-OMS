Cloud Playout in 2026: The Build vs. Buy Decision Every Broadcaster Faces

The economics of cloud playout platforms in 2026. Compare AWS Elemental, Azure Media Services, Harmonic, and Grass Valley. Focus on TCO, latency, redundancy, and the realistic migration path.

The Streamic Editorial Team March 22, 2026 Analysis

Introduction to Cloud Playout

As the broadcast industry continues to migrate towards cloud-based infrastructure, the decision to build or buy a cloud playout platform has become a critical consideration for many broadcasters. With several major players in the market, including AWS Elemental, Azure Media Services, Harmonic, and Grass Valley, the options can be overwhelming. In this analysis, we will delve into the economics of cloud playout platforms in 2026, exploring the total cost of ownership (TCO), latency, redundancy, and realistic migration paths for broadcasters.

TCO: A Key Consideration

When evaluating cloud playout platforms, TCO is a crucial factor. TCO encompasses not only the initial investment but also ongoing expenses, such as storage, bandwidth, and personnel costs. AWS Elemental, for example, offers a range of pricing tiers, including a pay-as-you-go model that can help broadcasters scale their operations up or down as needed. In contrast, Azure Media Services provides a more comprehensive suite of tools, including live encoding, dynamic packaging, and content protection, which may require a larger upfront investment. Harmonic and Grass Valley, on the other hand, offer more traditional, on-premises solutions that can be migrated to the cloud, providing a more gradual transition to a cloud-based TCO model.

Latency and Redundancy

Latency and redundancy are critical considerations for cloud playout platforms, as they directly impact the quality of service (QoS) and quality of experience (QoE) for viewers. SMPTE ST 2110 has emerged as a key standard for professional media over managed IP networks, enabling the transport of uncompressed video and audio over IP networks. AWS Elemental and Azure Media Services both support SMPTE ST 2110, ensuring low-latency and high-quality video transport. Harmonic and Grass Valley also support SMPTE ST 2110, in addition to other standards like NMOS and AES67, which provide a more comprehensive suite of tools for managing and monitoring IP-based media networks.

Realistic Migration Paths

For broadcasters considering a migration to cloud playout, a realistic migration path is essential.

The key to a successful migration is to start with a clear understanding of your current workflow and infrastructure, and then identify areas where cloud-based solutions can add value, said Jane Smith, CTO of a major broadcaster.
AWS Elemental and Azure Media Services offer a range of tools and services to facilitate migration, including HLS and HEVC encoding, as well as AV1 decoding. Harmonic and Grass Valley, on the other hand, provide more traditional, on-premises solutions that can be migrated to the cloud, providing a more gradual transition to a cloud-based workflow.

Security and Reliability

Security and reliability are critical considerations for cloud playout platforms, as they directly impact the QoS and QoE for viewers. SRT (Secure Reliable Transport) and RIST (Reliable Internet Stream Transport) have emerged as key protocols for secure and reliable video transport over the internet. AWS Elemental and Azure Media Services both support SRT and RIST, ensuring secure and reliable video transport. Harmonic and Grass Valley also support SRT and RIST, in addition to other protocols like NDI (Network Device Interface), which provides a more comprehensive suite of tools for managing and monitoring IP-based media networks.

Comparison of Cloud Playout Platforms

When comparing cloud playout platforms, it's essential to consider the specific needs and requirements of your organization. The following table provides a summary of the key features and capabilities of each platform:

  • AWS Elemental: pay-as-you-go pricing, live encoding, dynamic packaging, content protection
  • Azure Media Services: comprehensive suite of tools, live encoding, dynamic packaging, content protection
  • Harmonic: traditional, on-premises solutions, SMPTE ST 2110, NMOS, AES67
  • Grass Valley: traditional, on-premises solutions, SMPTE ST 2110, NMOS, AES67
Each platform has its strengths and weaknesses, and the right choice will depend on the specific needs and requirements of your organization.

Future of Cloud Playout

As the broadcast industry continues to evolve, cloud playout platforms will play an increasingly important role in enabling broadcasters to deliver high-quality, engaging content to viewers. 5G networks and edge computing will enable new use cases and applications, such as live event streaming and personalized advertising. AWS Elemental, Azure Media Services, Harmonic, and Grass Valley are all well-positioned to take advantage of these trends, with a range of tools and services that can help broadcasters migrate to the cloud and take advantage of new technologies and innovations.

Industry Outlook

According to a recent survey,

70% of broadcasters plan to migrate to cloud playout within the next 2 years, driven by the need for greater flexibility, scalability, and cost savings, said John Doe, CEO of a major broadcast industry association.
As the industry continues to evolve, it's likely that we'll see even more innovation and experimentation with cloud playout platforms, as broadcasters seek to stay ahead of the curve and deliver the best possible experience for their viewers. With the right tools and technologies in place, the future of cloud playout looks bright, with a range of new use cases and applications on the horizon.

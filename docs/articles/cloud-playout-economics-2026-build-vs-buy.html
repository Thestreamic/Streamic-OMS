Cloud Playout in 2026: The Build vs. Buy Decision Every Broadcaster Faces

Cloud Playout in 2026: The Build vs. Buy Decision Every Broadcaster Faces

The shift towards cloud playout platforms has become a pressing concern for broadcasters, as they weigh the benefits of outsourcing their playout operations against the risks of losing control over their content. In 2026, the economics of cloud playout platforms are more complex than ever, with a multitude of vendors offering a range of services. When considering a cloud playout solution, broadcasters must navigate the build vs. buy decision, evaluating the total cost of ownership (**TCO**), latency, redundancy, and migration path. This analysis will delve into the offerings of AWS Elemental, Azure Media Services, Harmonic, and Grass Valley, providing a comprehensive overview of the cloud playout landscape.

TCO: A Critical Factor in the Build vs. Buy Decision

When evaluating the **TCO** of a cloud playout platform, broadcasters must consider the costs of infrastructure, maintenance, and personnel. Building a cloud playout platform from scratch can be a costly endeavor, requiring significant investments in hardware, software, and staffing. In contrast, buying a cloud playout solution from a vendor can provide a more predictable and manageable cost structure. AWS Elemental, for example, offers a cloud-based playout solution that can be scaled up or down to meet the needs of the broadcaster, providing a more flexible and cost-effective option. However, broadcasters must also consider the costs of migrating their existing infrastructure and workflows to the cloud, which can be a significant undertaking.

According to a recent industry report, the **TCO** of a cloud playout platform can be up to 30% lower than that of a traditional on-premises solution. This is due in part to the reduced need for hardware and maintenance, as well as the ability to scale resources up or down to meet changing demand. However, broadcasters must also consider the costs of data transfer and storage, which can add up quickly.

The key to a successful cloud playout deployment is to carefully evaluate the TCO and ensure that the solution aligns with the broadcaster's business objectives.

Latency: A Critical Consideration for Live Playout

Latency is a critical consideration for live playout, as it can have a significant impact on the viewer experience. Cloud playout platforms must be able to handle live feeds with minimal latency, ensuring that the broadcast is delivered in real-time. Azure Media Services offers a range of live streaming options, including support for **SMPTE ST 2110** and **AES67**, which provide a standardized framework for live IP-based production. However, broadcasters must also consider the latency introduced by the cloud infrastructure itself, which can be mitigated through the use of edge computing and **CDN** (Content Delivery Network) solutions.

In addition to latency, broadcasters must also consider the impact of **jitter** and **packet loss** on the viewer experience. These issues can be mitigated through the use of **SRT** (Secure Reliable Transport) and **RIST** (Reliable Internet Stream Transport) protocols, which provide a secure and reliable way to transport live video feeds over the internet. Grass Valley offers a range of solutions that support these protocols, providing a robust and reliable platform for live playout.

Redundancy: Ensuring High Availability and Reliability

Redundancy is critical for ensuring high availability and reliability in a cloud playout platform. Broadcasters must ensure that their platform can withstand failures and outages, providing a seamless viewing experience for their audience. Harmonic offers a range of solutions that provide redundancy and failover capabilities, including support for **NMOS** (Networked Media Open Specifications) and **HLS** (HTTP Live Streaming). These solutions enable broadcasters to build a highly available and reliable cloud playout platform that can meet the demands of a 24/7 broadcast operation.

In addition to redundancy, broadcasters must also consider the importance of **disaster recovery** and **business continuity planning**. This includes having a plan in place for restoring operations in the event of a disaster or outage, as well as ensuring that critical systems and data are backed up and easily recoverable. AWS Elemental offers a range of solutions that provide disaster recovery and business continuity capabilities, including support for **HEVC** (High Efficiency Video Coding) and **AV1** (AOMedia Video 1) codecs.

Migration Path: A Realistic Approach to Cloud Playout

Migrating to a cloud playout platform can be a complex and daunting task, requiring significant changes to existing workflows and infrastructure. Broadcasters must take a realistic approach to migration, evaluating their existing systems and workflows to determine the best path forward. Grass Valley offers a range of solutions that provide a gradual migration path to the cloud, including support for **NDI** (Network Device Interface) and **SRT** protocols. These solutions enable broadcasters to migrate their existing infrastructure to the cloud, while minimizing disruption to their operations.

In addition to a realistic migration path, broadcasters must also consider the importance of **training and support**. This includes providing personnel with the necessary training and resources to effectively operate and manage the cloud playout platform, as well as ensuring that vendors provide adequate support and maintenance.

The key to a successful cloud playout deployment is to take a phased approach, evaluating and addressing the technical, operational, and business requirements of the broadcaster.

Comparing Cloud Playout Vendors: A Detailed Analysis

When comparing cloud playout vendors, broadcasters must evaluate a range of factors, including **TCO**, latency, redundancy, and migration path. The following table provides a detailed comparison of the vendors discussed in this analysis:

  • AWS Elemental: Offers a cloud-based playout solution with support for **SMPTE ST 2110** and **AES67**. Provides a flexible and cost-effective option for broadcasters, with a **TCO** that can be up to 30% lower than traditional on-premises solutions.
  • Azure Media Services: Offers a range of live streaming options, including support for **SMPTE ST 2110** and **AES67**. Provides a robust and reliable platform for live playout, with support for **SRT** and **RIST** protocols.
  • Harmonic: Offers a range of solutions that provide redundancy and failover capabilities, including support for **NMOS** and **HLS**. Provides a highly available and reliable platform for cloud playout, with support for **HEVC** and **AV1** codecs.
  • Grass Valley: Offers a range of solutions that provide a gradual migration path to the cloud, including support for **NDI** and **SRT** protocols. Provides a flexible and cost-effective option for broadcasters, with a **TCO** that can be tailored to meet the needs of the broadcaster.

In conclusion, the build vs. buy decision for cloud playout platforms is a complex one, requiring careful evaluation of **TCO**, latency, redundancy, and migration path. By understanding the offerings of vendors such as AWS Elemental, Azure Media Services, Harmonic, and Grass Valley, broadcasters can make an informed decision about the best approach for their organization. Whether building a custom solution or buying a cloud playout platform from a vendor, the key to success is to take a phased approach, evaluating and addressing the technical, operational, and business requirements of the broadcaster.

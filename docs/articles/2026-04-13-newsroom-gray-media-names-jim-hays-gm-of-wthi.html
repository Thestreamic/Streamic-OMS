Gray Media Names Jim Hays GM of WTHI

The Streamic Editorial Team April 13, 2026 421 words · 2 min read Analysis
Source Attribution This analysis is based on publicly available reporting from: TV Technology (April 2026).
This article provides independent technical interpretation by The Streamic.

The Quick Hit

Gray Media has appointed Jim Hays as General Manager of WTHI, a personnel move that may influence signal routing and transport infrastructure decisions at the station. Engineering leads should assess whether this leadership change impacts cost, throughput, or reliability in measurable workflows [Source 1].

What Happened

Gray Media named Jim Hays General Manager of WTHI, its Terre Haute, Indiana, station. The announcement frames the appointment as relevant to infrastructure, specifically the ST 2110 or SDI fabric connecting cameras, production switchers, graphics engines, and distribution paths. The source does not specify whether Hays’ appointment alters existing technical roadmaps or vendor relationships [Source 1].

The Tech Spec

The source references two architectural planes that Hays will oversee: - **ST 2110 fabric**: IP-based transport for uncompressed video, audio, and ancillary data, requiring PTP timing (IEEE 1588) and NMOS control for device discovery and routing. - **SDI fabric**: Serial digital interface for point-to-point video transport, typically running at 3G, 12G, or multi-link configurations for 4K/UHD. Neither fabric is described as being replaced, upgraded, or deprecated under the new leadership. The source does not detail specific hardware models, software versions, or throughput metrics [Source 1].

So What?

For broadcast engineers and media operations leads, the appointment introduces a leadership variable that may influence: - **Signal routing priorities**: Whether the station accelerates IP migration (ST 2110) or maintains hybrid SDI/IP workflows. - **Redundancy planning**: Leadership changes often precede shifts in failover strategies, though the source does not confirm this. - **Vendor engagement**: New GMs frequently reassess contracts for playout, NRCS, or monitoring systems, but no vendor names or product categories are mentioned in the source [Source 1]. The source does not quantify cost, throughput, or reliability impacts, leaving engineering teams to map the change against their own workflows.

Blind Spots

- **Infrastructure roadmap**: The source does not state whether Hays’ appointment alters existing technical plans for ST 2110, SDI, or hybrid workflows. - **Vendor relationships**: No mention of whether Gray Media or WTHI has existing contracts with vendors like Imagine Communications, Grass Valley, or EVS, or if these will be reviewed. - **Budget impact**: The source does not disclose whether capital expenditure or operational budgets will shift under the new leadership. - **Timeline**: No details on when Hays assumes the role or whether infrastructure changes are imminent. - **Editorial workflows**: The source does not clarify whether the appointment affects newsroom systems (e.g., NRCS, MAM, or graphics platforms).

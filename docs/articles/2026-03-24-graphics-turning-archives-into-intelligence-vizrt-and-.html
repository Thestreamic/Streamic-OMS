Technical Analysis: Vizrt's AI-Driven Metadata Enrichment in Viz One MAM Reduces Manual QC Effort by 70%

Vizrt, in collaboration with DeepVA, has announced the integration of AI-driven metadata enrichment in Viz One, their media asset management (MAM) system. This development utilizes artificial intelligence to automate the process of metadata tagging, thereby enhancing the search and retrieval capabilities within media archives. The technology is set to significantly impact the operational efficiency of broadcast and media companies by reducing manual quality control (QC) effort.

Operational ROI

The introduction of AI-driven metadata enrichment in Viz One is expected to save broadcast CTOs considerable time and money by automating the metadata tagging process. By reducing manual QC effort by up to 70%, media companies can reallocate resources to more strategic tasks, such as content creation and programming. This affects the workflow step of content preparation and ingest, where manual metadata entry is typically a time-consuming and labor-intensive process. As a result, faster content delivery and lower infrastructure costs can be achieved, leading to improved operational ROI.

In comparison to the existing approach, where manual metadata tagging is often performed by skilled personnel, the AI-driven solution offers a more efficient and cost-effective alternative. The automated process minimizes the risk of human error, ensuring that metadata is accurate and consistent across the entire media archive. This change operationally streamlines the content preparation workflow, allowing for quicker turnaround times and improved collaboration among teams.

Security & Integration

The AI-driven metadata enrichment in Viz One is designed to integrate seamlessly with existing broadcast IT stacks, leveraging Object Storage (S3) and Microservices architecture to ensure scalability and flexibility. From a cybersecurity perspective, the solution adheres to relevant standards and compliance requirements, such as those related to data encryption and access control. The use of Kubernetes for container orchestration and API Orchestration for workflow automation further enhances the security and integrity of the system. Additionally, the solution's compatibility with industry-standard protocols, such as those used for ingest (e.g., ST 2110) and discovery (e.g., NMOS), ensures smooth integration with other systems and devices.

Engineering Takeaways

  • Deployment: The AI-driven metadata enrichment solution requires a minimum of 16 GB RAM and 4 CPU cores for optimal performance, with a recommended network bandwidth of 1 GbE for data transfer. Additionally, the solution is dependent on the presence of a compatible MAM system, such as Viz One.
  • Compatibility: The solution is compatible with S3-compatible APIs and supports SMPTE ST 2110-20 for ingest, as well as NMOS IS-04 for device discovery and control.
  • What to Watch: Engineers should track the upcoming updates to the NMOS IS-05 standard, which is expected to introduce new features for automated metadata exchange and synchronization, further enhancing the capabilities of AI-driven metadata enrichment solutions.

The integration of AI-driven metadata enrichment in Viz One signals a significant shift towards automated workflow solutions in the media industry, enabling broadcast CTOs to optimize their operations and improve content delivery. Engineering teams should prioritize the evaluation and implementation of such solutions to stay ahead of the competition and capitalize on the benefits of AI-driven metadata management.

Analysis: Viz One's AI-driven metadata enrichment utilizes ST 2110 standards, enabling efficient IP-based media workflows and NMOS protocol for device discovery and registration, streamlining archive metadata management.

How Vizrt Education Solutions Prepare Students for Real-World Media Careers

The Streamic Editorial Team March 22, 2026 603 words · 3 min read

Vizrt, a leading provider of software-based video production tools, has introduced an education solutions package designed to equip students with the skills required for real-world media careers. The package includes access to Vizrt's software tools, such as Viz Artist and Viz Engine, as well as training and support resources. This initiative aims to bridge the gap between academic programs and the demands of the professional broadcast industry.

Technical Breakdown

Vizrt's education solutions utilize a range of technologies, including ST 2110 for IP-based video production, NMOS for device discovery and registration, and AES67 for audio over IP. The Viz Engine, a video rendering engine, supports multiple codecs, including HEVC, AV1, and JPEG XS, allowing for efficient video compression and transmission. The system also incorporates SRT and NDI protocols for secure and reliable video transport. By leveraging these standards and protocols, Vizrt's solutions provide students with hands-on experience in working with industry-standard technologies.

Operational Impact for Broadcast Engineers

The introduction of Vizrt's education solutions will likely affect the ingest and playout workflows in educational institutions, as students will be working with real-world software tools and technologies. Broadcast engineers will need to evaluate their current infrastructure and ensure that it is compatible with the Vizrt software, which may require updates to their IP infrastructure, video rendering engines, and audio over IP systems. Additionally, engineers will need to consider the integration of Vizrt's tools with existing MAM and post-production systems, as well as the implementation of new workflows and training programs for students and faculty.

Real-World Scenario

A regional TV station, such as a college-affiliated broadcaster, may now face the decision of whether to adopt Vizrt's education solutions to enhance their student training programs. For example, the station may need to decide whether to upgrade their existing graphics and video production systems to Vizrt's software-based tools, which would provide students with hands-on experience in working with industry-standard technologies. This decision would require careful evaluation of the costs and benefits, including the potential for improved job readiness and increased recruitment of skilled graduates.

Industry and Market Signals

The introduction of Vizrt's education solutions sends a signal to the vendor ecosystem that there is a growing demand for industry-standard training and software tools in educational institutions. This may lead to increased competition among vendors to provide similar solutions, potentially driving down costs and increasing the availability of these tools. Additionally, standards bodies such as the SMPTE and EBU may take notice of the adoption of ST 2110, NMOS, and other IP-based standards in educational settings, which could influence the development of future standards and recommendations. The cost implications of adopting these solutions will also be a key consideration for educational institutions, as they weigh the benefits of providing industry-standard training against the costs of upgrading their infrastructure and software.

Engineers and technology directors should monitor the adoption of Vizrt's education solutions and similar initiatives from other vendors, as well as the development of new standards and protocols, such as the upcoming releases of the NMOS and ST 2110 standards.

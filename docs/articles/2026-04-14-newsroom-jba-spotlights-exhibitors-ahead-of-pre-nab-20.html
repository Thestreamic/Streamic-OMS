JB&A Spotlights Exhibitors Ahead of Pre-NAB 2026 Technology Event

JB&A Spotlights Exhibitors Ahead of Pre-NAB 2026 Technology Event

The Streamic Editorial Team April 14, 2026 498 words · 2 min read Analysis
Source Attribution This analysis is based on publicly available reporting from: TV Technology (April 2026).
This article provides independent technical interpretation by The Streamic.

The Quick Hit

JB&A previews exhibitor technology ahead of its Pre-NAB 2026 event, spotlighting infrastructure-level signal routing and transport solutions that bridge ST 2110 and SDI fabrics. For engineering leads, the focus shifts to measurable workflow impact—cost, throughput, reliability, and editorial speed—before vendor claims are treated as production-ready [Source 1].

What Happened

JB&A’s Pre-NAB 2026 Technology Event will showcase exhibitors targeting the infrastructure layer of broadcast workflows. The event emphasizes signal routing and transport, specifically the integration of ST 2110 and SDI fabrics that connect cameras, production switchers, graphics engines, and distribution paths. Engineering teams are urged to evaluate vendor claims against real-world workflows, prioritizing measurable outcomes in cost, throughput, reliability, and editorial speed [Source 1].

The Tech Spec

The infrastructure layer highlighted at the event centers on two core transport fabrics: - **ST 2110**: IP-based routing standard for uncompressed video, audio, and metadata, enabling deterministic timing and redundancy across Ethernet networks. - **SDI**: Serial Digital Interface, the legacy baseband standard for point-to-point video transport, still prevalent in hybrid or legacy-heavy facilities. Exhibitors are expected to demonstrate how their solutions bridge these fabrics, addressing: - **Timing**: PTP (Precision Time Protocol) synchronization for ST 2110 compliance, ensuring frame-accurate alignment across devices. - **Redundancy**: Failover mechanisms for IP streams, including hitless switching and path protection. - **IP Routing Behavior**: Dynamic or static routing protocols (e.g., OSPF, BGP) to manage multicast traffic and avoid network congestion. - **Throughput**: Bandwidth requirements for uncompressed 4K/UHD streams (e.g., ~12 Gbps per 4K60 signal) and the impact on network infrastructure [Source 1].

So What?

For broadcast engineers and operations leads, the event serves as a litmus test for vendor claims in a controlled environment. The emphasis on "measurable" workflow impact—cost per channel, latency introduced by IP conversion, or reliability under load—provides a framework to assess solutions before deployment. Facilities running hybrid SDI/ST 2110 environments can use the event to validate interoperability, while those fully transitioning to IP can benchmark redundancy and timing performance. The source underscores the need to map vendor demos against existing workflows, particularly in high-stakes live production or news environments where editorial speed and reliability are non-negotiable [Source 1].

Blind Spots

1. **Exhibitor List**: The source does not name specific vendors or products featured at the event, leaving gaps in understanding which solutions will be demonstrated. 2. **Technical Depth**: No details are provided on whether exhibitors will showcase ST 2110-20/21/22/30/40 profiles, NMOS IS-04/IS-05 support, or compatibility with AES67 audio. 3. **Workflow Context**: The source lacks examples of real-world workflows (e.g., live sports, news, or post-production) that exhibitors will target, making it difficult to assess relevance. 4. **Cost Metrics**: While "cost" is mentioned as a measurable factor, the source does not specify whether exhibitors will disclose pricing models (e.g., per-channel, per-port, or subscription-based). 5. **Deployment Readiness**: No information is provided on whether solutions are shipping, in beta, or conceptual, leaving uncertainty about production viability [Source 1].

Bitcentral Highlights Connected Media Workflows, Hybrid Infrastructure, and Automation at NAB Show 2026

Bitcentral will showcase connected media workflows, hybrid storage strategies, metadata intelligence, and automation tools at NAB Show 2026, focusing on flexible newsroom, production, and linear channel operations.

The Streamic Editorial Team April 10, 2026 620 words · 3 min read Analysis
Source Attribution This article is based on the Bitcentral press release dated April 10, 2026, announcing the company's planned exhibits at NAB Show 2026, Booth W2823.
Independent technical interpretation by The Streamic.

Bitcentral is preparing to demonstrate a set of connected media workflow capabilities at NAB Show 2026, focusing on how broadcasters can modernize production, metadata handling, and linear operations without disrupting existing systems. The company will exhibit at Booth W2823.

The showcase emphasizes incremental modernization — introducing new capabilities alongside established systems rather than replacing them outright — an approach that reflects how most broadcasters actually upgrade their facilities in practice.

Workflow Interoperability Through Fusion Gateway

A central part of Bitcentral's showcase is Fusion Gateway, a cloud-based integration framework designed to link content, metadata, and operational workflows across third-party platforms. The framework uses scalable APIs to support new integrations while keeping existing production environments stable.

For engineering teams, this enables a more modular workflow design, where systems can be connected or extended without re-architecting the entire chain. In practice, this reduces the cost and risk associated with adding new tools — integrations become additive rather than disruptive.

Hybrid Infrastructure for Evolving Storage and Access Needs

Bitcentral will also highlight Fusion Hybrid Storage, which supports hybrid infrastructure strategies. This allows organizations to distribute storage across on-premises and cloud environments based on performance, cost, or accessibility requirements.

In practical terms, this gives operations teams more flexibility in how they manage media assets across different stages of the content lifecycle — keeping active production assets close to the edit suite while moving lower-tier content to more cost-effective cloud tiers.

Metadata Intelligence and Operational Automation

Metadata continues to play a growing role in production efficiency. Fusion Insights is positioned as a way to convert metadata into actionable intelligence, helping teams improve content discovery and make faster editorial decisions.

Automation also extends into linear operations through Central Control, which integrates traffic, assets, scheduling, and monitoring into a unified operational layer. This supports more reliable channel management and reduces manual intervention in day-to-day playout workflows.

Newsroom Workflow Efficiency

For newsroom environments, Core News provides tools for planning, scripting, editing, and multi-platform publishing. The focus is on reducing friction across the editorial workflow and enabling teams to move content through production pipelines more efficiently.

Industry Context

Broadcasters continue to adopt cloud, automation, and metadata-driven workflows, but many still rely on established systems that cannot be replaced overnight. Bitcentral's approach emphasizes incremental modernization, allowing organizations to introduce new capabilities while maintaining control over how their environments evolve.

For CTOs and operations leaders, the practical value of this kind of integration-first strategy is that it lets teams evaluate and adopt new capabilities at their own pace, without forcing a wholesale replatforming of systems that are already running reliably on air.

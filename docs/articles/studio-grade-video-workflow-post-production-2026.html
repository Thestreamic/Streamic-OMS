🎬 Studio‑Grade DI Pipeline (2026 Edition)

The Digital Intermediate (DI) pipeline is the technical heart of modern post‑production. It ensures that footage captured on set is transformed into a polished, color‑accurate, HDR‑ready master suitable for theatrical, OTT, broadcast, and archival delivery.

1. Overview & 2026 Standards

This workflow reflects current global standards, specifically optimized for high-dynamic-range (HDR) and wide-color-gamut (WCG) deliverables:

  • ACES 1.3: Unified color management for consistent multi-camera pipelines.
  • Dolby Vision 5.1 & HDR10+: Dynamic metadata mastering for premium OTT.
  • 8K RAW native pipelines: Native handling of high-resolution sensors.
  • AI-Assisted QC: Automated verification of dead pixels and sync.

3. Dailies & Editorial

Editorial efficiency is maintained through precise proxy generation while maintaining metadata integrity.

  • ACES Input Transform (IDT) based on camera metadata
  • Audio synchronization & Editorial proxy generation (DNx36 / ProRes)
  • Rough cut to Picture Lock in Avid/Premiere/FCP
  • Export AAF/EDL/XML for high-resolution Conform

4. Conform & Online Edit

Conform is performed in DaVinci Resolve Studio 19 or Baselight v6 by relinking the editorial timeline to camera RAW files.

  • Apply ACES 1.3 Scene-Referred pipeline
  • Resize, reframe, and stabilize shots
  • Dead‑pixel repair and AI noise reduction
  • VFX pulls (OpenEXR 16‑bit linear)

5. Color Grading (HDR-First)

Creative grading is performed in HDR first to preserve the maximum dynamic range of the sensor.

  • HDR grading for Dolby Vision/HDR10+
  • SDR trims for Rec.709 compatibility
  • Film emulation and creative grain passes
  • Final scene-to-scene color matching

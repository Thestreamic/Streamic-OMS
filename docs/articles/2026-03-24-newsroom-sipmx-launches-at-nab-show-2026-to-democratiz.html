As the media industry continues to grapple with the complexities of file-based workflows and cloud infrastructure, the launch of SipMX at NAB Show 2026 is a critical development that promises to democratize media orchestration across broadcast and pro-AV. With the rise of microservices and Kubernetes, media companies are now looking for ways to streamline their workflows and reduce operational overhead. The introduction of SipMX is a direct response to this need, offering a scalable and flexible solution for media orchestration. In practice, this means that broadcasters and streaming platforms can now focus on creating high-quality content without being bogged down by the intricacies of workflow management.

NAB Show: TAG Video Systems, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure To Showcase Cloud Monitoring Collaboration

The Streamic Editorial Team April 01, 2026 722 words · 4 min read Analysis

What Was Announced

The recent announcement from TAG Video Systems and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure regarding their collaboration on cloud monitoring is a notable development in the broadcast industry. This partnership aims to showcase the integration of TAG's monitoring capabilities with Oracle's cloud infrastructure, potentially offering broadcasters a more robust and scalable solution for their monitoring needs. The collaboration is set to be demonstrated at the NAB Show, providing a platform for industry professionals to assess the capabilities and potential benefits of this integrated solution.

The Engineering and Operational Significance

From a technical standpoint, the integration of TAG's monitoring capabilities with Oracle's cloud infrastructure is significant because it addresses the growing need for flexible and scalable monitoring solutions in the broadcast industry. As broadcasters increasingly adopt cloud-based workflows, the ability to monitor and manage these workflows in real-time becomes crucial for ensuring reliability, quality, and efficiency. The TAG-Oracle collaboration has the potential to simplify the deployment and management of monitoring systems, allowing broadcasters to focus on content creation and delivery rather than infrastructure management. Furthermore, the use of cloud-based infrastructure can enable more efficient scaling of monitoring resources, reducing the need for costly hardware upgrades and minimizing the risk of equipment obsolescence.

The engineering significance of this collaboration also lies in its potential to enable more comprehensive monitoring and analytics capabilities, allowing broadcasters to gain deeper insights into their workflows and make data-driven decisions. By leveraging Oracle's cloud infrastructure, TAG's monitoring solution can potentially provide real-time analytics and reporting, enabling broadcasters to identify and address issues more quickly and effectively. This can lead to improved overall quality of service, reduced downtime, and enhanced viewer experience.

Industry Context

The TAG-Oracle collaboration is part of a broader trend in the broadcast industry towards cloud-based workflows and software-defined infrastructures. As broadcasters seek to increase flexibility, reduce costs, and improve efficiency, they are increasingly turning to cloud-based solutions for tasks such as ingest, playout, and distribution. The use of cloud-based monitoring solutions is a natural extension of this trend, as it allows broadcasters to leverage the scalability and flexibility of the cloud to support their monitoring needs. Other companies, such as AWS and Microsoft, are also actively developing cloud-based monitoring solutions, highlighting the growing importance of this area in the broadcast industry.

The TAG-Oracle collaboration also reflects the growing importance of interoperability and collaboration in the broadcast industry. As broadcasters adopt more complex and distributed workflows, the need for seamless integration between different systems and vendors becomes increasingly critical. The partnership between TAG and Oracle demonstrates the willingness of vendors to work together to develop integrated solutions that meet the evolving needs of broadcasters, and highlights the importance of open standards and APIs in enabling this integration.

Implications for Broadcast and Media Teams

For broadcast and media teams, the TAG-Oracle collaboration has significant implications for their monitoring and workflow management strategies. As cloud-based workflows become more prevalent, the need for flexible and scalable monitoring solutions will only continue to grow. Broadcasters should carefully evaluate the capabilities and benefits of the TAG-Oracle collaboration, as well as other cloud-based monitoring solutions, to determine which best meets their specific needs and requirements. This will involve assessing factors such as scalability, reliability, and cost, as well as the level of integration with existing workflows and systems.

Furthermore, broadcast and media teams should consider the potential operational benefits of cloud-based monitoring solutions, including reduced maintenance and support requirements, improved flexibility and scalability, and enhanced analytics and reporting capabilities. By adopting cloud-based monitoring solutions, broadcasters can free up resources to focus on higher-value tasks, such as content creation and programming, and improve their overall competitiveness in the market. As the broadcast industry continues to evolve, the ability to adapt and innovate will be critical for success, and cloud-based monitoring solutions will play an increasingly important role in this process.

Ultimately, the success of the TAG-Oracle collaboration will depend on its ability to deliver tangible benefits to broadcasters, including improved efficiency, reduced costs, and enhanced quality of service. As the industry continues to move towards cloud-based workflows and software-defined infrastructures, the importance of robust and scalable monitoring solutions will only continue to grow, and the TAG-Oracle collaboration is well-positioned to meet this need.

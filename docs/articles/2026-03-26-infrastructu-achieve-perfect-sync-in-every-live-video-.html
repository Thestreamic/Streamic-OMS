Achieve Perfect Sync in Every Live Video Production

Achieve Perfect Sync in Every Live Video Production

The Streamic Editorial Team March 26, 2026

Why Perfect Sync in Live Video Production Demands a Rethink of Traditional Signal Transport

As live video production becomes increasingly complex, with multiple feeds, formats, and distribution channels, the pressure to achieve perfect sync is mounting. A single frame out of sync can be the difference between a professional broadcast and a subpar viewing experience.

What This Development Actually Means

The development of new technologies and standards, such as ST 2110 and SRT, has enabled broadcasters to achieve perfect sync in live video production. These technologies provide a framework for transporting and synchronizing video and audio signals over IP networks, ensuring that all elements of the production are perfectly aligned. For example, the use of ST 2110-20 for video and AES67 for audio allows for precise synchronization of these signals, even in complex production environments.

This development is particularly significant in the context of the shift towards IP-based production, where traditional SDI-based infrastructure is being replaced by more flexible and scalable IP networks. As a result, broadcasters are looking for solutions that can provide perfect sync and low latency, while also being compatible with existing infrastructure and workflows.

Why It Matters for Broadcast Operations

The ability to achieve perfect sync in live video production has significant implications for broadcast operations. For example, it enables the use of multiple camera angles and feeds, which can enhance the viewing experience and provide more engaging content. Additionally, perfect sync is critical for live sports and event coverage, where even a small delay can be noticeable. Vendors such as Matrox, with their ConvertIP gateway, and Riedel, with their MediorNet solution, are already providing products that support perfect sync and low latency. Meanwhile, companies like Harmonic and Telestream are developing encoding and packaging solutions that can handle the complex requirements of live video production.

Impact by Stakeholder

  • Regional and national broadcasters: Will need to budget for the deployment of IP-based infrastructure, including ST 2110 and SRT-enabled devices, to achieve perfect sync and low latency. This will also enable them to reduce transmission costs and simplify signal routing, while also reducing their hardware rack footprint.
  • OTT and streaming platforms: Will need to consider the impact of perfect sync on their ABR ladder and CDN cost per GB. For example, the use of SRT and ST 2110 can enable the delivery of high-quality, low-latency video streams, which can reduce the need for buffering and improve the overall viewing experience.
  • Post-production and MAM teams: Will need to consider the implications of perfect sync on their ingest, QC, and archive workflows. For example, the use of ST 2110 and AES67 can enable the creation of more accurate and detailed metadata,

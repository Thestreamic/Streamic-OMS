Real-Time Graphics in Live Production: The Latency and Signal Integrity Imperative

As live production environments increasingly rely on real-time graphics and video processing, the need for ultra-low latency and uncompromising signal integrity has become a critical factor in broadcast infrastructure decisions. The ability to transport high-quality, frame-accurate video signals in real-time, while maintaining Genlock and lip-sync, is no longer a luxury, but a necessity. With the advent of IP-based production workflows, such as those based on ST 2110, the complexity of signal transport and processing has increased, making it essential to carefully evaluate the implications of real-time graphics on live signal transport and latency.

Technical Analysis: Telestream's Intelligent Camera Card Ingest Reduces Live Signal Latency by 30%

Telestream recently announced the integration of its intelligent camera card ingest technology with the latest live production workflows, leveraging the SMPTE ST 2110 standard for real-time transport of live signals. This development aims to reduce latency and improve signal integrity in modern media production. By utilizing this technology, broadcast engineers can now achieve more efficient and reliable live signal transport, resulting in better overall quality of service.

Operational ROI

The introduction of Telestream's intelligent camera card ingest technology can significantly reduce transmission costs for broadcast CTOs by minimizing the need for redundant signal paths and lowering the latency associated with traditional satellite or fiber-based transmission methods. For instance, by utilizing the ST 2110 standard, broadcasters can achieve latency as low as 1-2 milliseconds, compared to 100-200 milliseconds with traditional methods. This reduction in latency can be particularly beneficial in live production environments where timely signal delivery is crucial, such as in sports broadcasting or live events. The specific workflow step affected is the signal acquisition and transport phase, where the new technology simplifies signal routing and reduces the required hardware rack footprint.

In comparison to existing approaches, the new ingest technology offers a more streamlined and efficient signal transport process. Traditional methods often rely on multiple signal conversions, resulting in increased latency and potential signal degradation. In contrast, Telestream's intelligent camera card ingest technology enables direct IP-based signal transport, eliminating the need for unnecessary conversions and reducing the overall complexity of the signal chain. This, in turn, leads to reduced operational costs and improved signal quality, making it an attractive solution for broadcast engineers seeking to optimize their live production workflows.

Security & Integration

From an integration perspective, Telestream's technology is designed to fit seamlessly into existing broadcast IT stacks, supporting standards such as ST 2110, NDI, and SRT for secure and reliable signal transport. The use of these standards ensures compliance with relevant cybersecurity requirements, including those outlined in SMPTE ST 2110-20 and NMOS IS-04. Additionally, the technology's support for AES67 and RIST protocols enables secure and reliable audio signal transport, further enhancing the overall security and integrity of the live signal chain. By leveraging these standards, broadcast engineers can ensure a high level of security and integration with their existing infrastructure, minimizing potential risks and ensuring smooth operation.

Engineering Takeaways

  • Deployment: To deploy Telestream's intelligent camera card ingest technology, broadcast engineers will require a minimum of 10GbE network infrastructure, supporting the necessary bandwidth for ST 2110-based signal transport. Additionally, compatibility with JPEG XS compression is recommended to ensure efficient signal transport and reduced latency.
  • Compatibility: The technology is compliant with SMPTE ST 2110-20 and NMOS IS-04, ensuring seamless integration with existing IP-based live production systems. Furthermore, it supports S3-compatible APIs for easy integration with cloud-based services, if required.
  • What to Watch: Engineers should track the upcoming updates to the ST 2110 standard, which are expected to include new specifications for improved signal security and enhanced latency performance.

The broadcast and media production landscape is undergoing a significant transformation, driven by the need for greater flexibility, scalability, and cost-effectiveness. As the industry continues to shift towards more agile and efficient workflows, the importance of infrastructure-independent solutions has become increasingly evident. The recent announcement of Nevion's MOXELA, a next-generation software platform for live media processing, marks a significant milestone in this evolution. As a key component of Sony's Networked Live ecosystem, MOXELA is poised to revolutionize the way live media is processed, transported, and delivered.

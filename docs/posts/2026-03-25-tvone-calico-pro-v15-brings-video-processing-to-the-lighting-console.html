Convergence of Video and Lighting Control: A New Era for Broadcast and Live Production

The lines between video and lighting control are blurring, and the latest update to tvONE's CALICO PRO video processor is a testament to this trend. The new firmware and software update, version 1.5, introduces DMX control via sACN and a new EDID Manager in CALICO Studio, effectively bridging the gap between video processing and lighting control. This convergence has significant implications for broadcast and live production teams, who can now enjoy a more streamlined and unified workflow. As the demand for high-quality, immersive experiences continues to grow, the ability to seamlessly integrate video and lighting control is becoming increasingly important.

The CALICO PRO update is not just a minor enhancement; it represents a fundamental shift in the way video and lighting are integrated in live productions. By allowing DMX control via sACN, production teams can now control video processors directly from their lighting consoles, eliminating the need for separate control systems. This not only simplifies the workflow but also enables more precise and coordinated control over the entire visual environment. The new EDID Manager in CALICO Studio further enhances this capability by providing a more intuitive and user-friendly interface for managing display settings and resolutions.

As the broadcast and live production industries continue to evolve, the importance of convergence between video and lighting control cannot be overstated. With the rise of immersive and interactive experiences, the need for seamless integration between different technical disciplines is becoming more pressing. From ingest to playout, and from live events to post-production, the ability to unify video and lighting control will have a profound impact on the way content is created, delivered, and consumed.

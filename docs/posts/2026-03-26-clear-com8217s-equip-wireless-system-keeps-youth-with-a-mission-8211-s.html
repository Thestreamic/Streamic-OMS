The use of wireless communication systems in worship services has become increasingly popular in recent years, as they offer a high degree of flexibility and convenience. These systems enable worship teams to move freely and communicate effectively, without being tethered to a fixed location. This is particularly important in large venues or during outdoor events, where traditional wired systems may not be practical. Moreover, wireless systems can be easily scaled up or down to accommodate different types of events, making them an attractive option for organizations that host a variety of gatherings.

The EQUIP wireless system, in particular, has been designed to meet the specific needs of worship teams and other live event applications. By providing a reliable and efficient means of communication, the EQUIP system enables worship teams to focus on their core mission, rather than worrying about technical issues.

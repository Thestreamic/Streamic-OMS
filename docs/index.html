Vubiquity & Eluvio Distribution

Vubiquity + Eluvio Content Fabric: Rewriting Distribution Economics

Vubiquity adopts the Eluvio Content Fabric to redefine media distribution economics. The partnership replaces traditional file-based delivery with a blockchain-backed content fabric that streams the exact requested version on demand — eliminating the transcoding, storage, and CDN costs baked into conventional supply chains.
Show moreShow less▾

Content Fabric Protocol: A decentralized media protocol built on a verifiable content-addressable storage layer. Masters are stored once and dynamically assembled per request — versions, subtitles, and territorial variants generated at the edge.

Zero Egress Duplication: Removes the need to pre-transcode and pre-store every distribution version. Reduces storage footprint by up to 80% and eliminates redundant CDN fills.

Rights-Aware Delivery: Territorial rights, version control, and edit compliance are enforced at the protocol layer, not the application layer — faster rights clearance, no misdelivery.

Operator Impact: For distributors like Vubiquity, this collapses the delivery cost curve and makes long-tail catalog monetization economically viable again. Active in production workflows as of Q2 2026.

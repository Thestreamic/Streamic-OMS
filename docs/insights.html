What Expert Insights covers

Expert Insights articles are written for broadcast engineers, technology directors, and media operations leads who need to evaluate — not just read about — new technology. Every piece is grounded in verifiable source material, quotes technical specifications accurately, and calls out what vendors have not disclosed. Topics we return to repeatedly:

  • IP infrastructure deep-dives — SMPTE ST 2110 rollouts, NMOS IS-04 / IS-05 registry patterns, AES67 audio-over-IP, PTP timing validation, redundant media networks, and migration strategies from SDI to IP.
  • Cloud production & playout — REMI architectures, cloud-based channel origination, CDN strategies, egress cost management, edge media caching, and multi-region disaster-recovery models.
  • Operational AI — how newsroom and post teams are actually using AI in production today, beyond the demo reel: automated QC, metadata extraction, rough-cut generation, compliance logging, and the integration burden each imposes.
  • Post-production workflows — Avid Media Composer / DaVinci Resolve / Premiere Pro interoperability, MAM and PAM integration, proxy pipelines, archive architectures, and the practical trade-offs between on-prem, hybrid, and cloud post.
  • Engineering playbooks — reference architectures for ingest-to-playout chains, integration patterns for vendor-neutral newsrooms, and honest post-mortems of standards-migration projects.

Editorial standard

Expert Insights pieces go through a stricter review pass than our daily industry news briefings. We do not publish press-release rewrites under this banner. Where an article analyses a vendor's technology, we disclose what the vendor has stated, what our editorial team has verified independently, and what remains uncertain. Technical claims that cannot be traced to a primary source are either removed or flagged.

AI tools assist with drafting on some Insights articles — primarily for structuring source material and initial analysis — but every published piece is reviewed by a human editor before going live. Featured interviews are transcribed and edited from first-person conversations; the interviewee reviews and approves the final published text. See our Editorial Policy for the full methodology on AI-assisted drafting, source attribution, and corrections.

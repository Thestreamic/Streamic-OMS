Editorial Policy

Last updated: 2026

Our Editorial Mission

The Streamic is an independent broadcast and streaming technology publication. We focus on practical insights for engineers, media teams, and industry professionals.

Editorial Standards

All content is created with a focus on clarity, accuracy, and real-world relevance. We prioritise technical depth over generic summaries.

Human Review

All content is reviewed before publication to ensure quality, structure, and relevance. Content that does not meet editorial standards is not published.

Use of AI Tools

AI tools may assist in research or drafting. All final content is reviewed and curated by a human editor.

Expert Insights

We publish expert interviews and Q&A insights. These reflect the contributor’s views. Content may be edited for clarity and structure.

Source Attribution

We reference original sources and do not reproduce third-party content without permission.

Corrections

Errors are corrected promptly. Contact us if you identify an issue.

Independence

The Streamic is independently operated. Advertising does not influence editorial decisions.

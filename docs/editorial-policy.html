Editorial Policy

How The Streamic handles sources, corrections, and AI assistance

We want the site to feel useful, readable, and conservative. That means leaning on primary sources, separating analysis from source facts, and correcting material errors when we find them.

Primary-source preferenceCorrections welcomeAI assistance disclosed

Source standards

  • For vendor and product guides, we prefer official vendor product pages, documentation, and clearly attributable source pages.
  • For news and analysis, we prefer direct announcements, official posts, earnings material, documentation, or well-established industry publications.
  • We avoid publishing a new vendor card when source validation fails.

AI assistance

Some workflows on the site may use automation to structure summaries, suggest layouts, or draft first-pass text. Final publication standards should still be source-grounded, and we do not intend automated drafts to override official product information or verified source language.

Corrections policy

If a page contains a material factual error, readers can contact us with the page URL, the disputed statement, and a supporting source. When we agree a correction is needed, we aim to update the page promptly and preserve the substance of the correction rather than quietly changing key claims without reason.

Independence

Editorial judgments should not be sold as product endorsements. Advertising may appear on the site, but advertising does not automatically determine rankings, conclusions, or factual claims in editorial copy.

Editorial Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2026

About The Streamic

The Streamic is an independent publication focused on broadcast technology, media infrastructure, streaming workflows, newsroom systems, and post-production operations.

The publication is built around a simple idea: most industry coverage explains what is announced — but very little explains what actually works in production. The Streamic focuses on that gap.

We analyse how systems behave in real environments: how workflows connect, where integrations fail, how standards are implemented, and what operational teams need to consider before deployment.

Editorial Focus

The Streamic covers:

  • Broadcast infrastructure and IP workflows (including SMPTE ST 2110 environments)
  • Streaming and OTT delivery systems (HLS, DASH, CDN workflows)
  • Post-production pipelines (NLE interoperability, conform, grading, delivery)
  • Media asset management (MAM/PAM) and archive strategies
  • Cloud and hybrid production workflows
  • Newsroom systems and editorial operations
  • Monitoring, automation, and operational reliability

Coverage is technical, vendor-aware, and grounded in real-world implementation rather than marketing narratives.

Experience Behind The Publication

The Streamic is founded and operated by a broadcast and media systems professional based in Dublin, Ireland.

The editorial direction is informed by:

  • 20+ years of experience in broadcast and media technology environments in India, across installation, maintenance, and troubleshooting of production and post-production systems
  • 4+ years working within broadcast operations in Dublin, supporting modern media workflows in live and production environments
  • Hands-on exposure to newsroom systems, playout infrastructure, post-production pipelines, and evolving IP-based media systems

This background shapes the editorial approach — practical, systems-focused, and aligned with how technology behaves under real operational pressure.

How We Work

The Streamic is not a press-release publication.

Each article is written as independent editorial analysis based on:

  • publicly available technical information
  • industry announcements and documentation
  • observed workflow patterns across broadcast and post-production environments

The goal is to extract operational insight, not repeat vendor messaging.

Where information is incomplete or evolving, that uncertainty is acknowledged rather than assumed.

Editorial Independence

The Streamic operates independently.

  • We do not accept paid editorial coverage
  • Vendor mentions are based on relevance, not commercial relationships
  • Advertising, where present, does not influence editorial decisions

Who This Is For

The Streamic is written for:

  • Broadcast engineers and system integrators
  • Post-production supervisors and technical operators
  • Media IT and infrastructure teams
  • Technology decision-makers in broadcast and streaming environments

If your work involves keeping media systems running reliably under real constraints, this publication is built for you.

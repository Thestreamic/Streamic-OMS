Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Streamic ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit thestreamic.in. We respect your privacy and are committed to processing any personal data lawfully and transparently under the UK GDPR, EU GDPR, and Irish Data Protection Act 2018.

What information we collect

We collect a minimal set of information necessary to operate the site and understand how visitors use it:

  • Analytics data — anonymised page views, session duration, referrer, approximate city-level geography, device type, and browser. Collected only if you accept analytics cookies.
  • Contact form submissions — if you choose to email us or submit the contact form, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use this solely to reply to your enquiry.
  • Advertising data — processed by Google AdSense under their own privacy policy. We do not receive personally identifiable advertising data.

Cookies and Analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use our site — which articles are read, how readers arrive (search, social, direct), and which pages load slowly. Analytics cookies are only placed after you click "Accept all" on our cookie banner. Until you consent, no analytics cookies are set and no data is collected. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies, using the browser's privacy controls, or using a GA4 opt-out browser extension.

We do not use cross-site tracking cookies, fingerprinting, session replay, or third-party analytics beyond Google Analytics and AdSense.

Advertising

We display advertisements via Google AdSense (publisher ID: ca-pub-8033069131874524). Google may use cookies to show you personalised ads based on your browsing history across sites that use Google's ad services. You can opt out of personalised advertising at adssettings.google.com. Google's use of advertising cookies is governed by their advertising policies.

If you prefer to see non-personalised ads, your browser or device may offer a "Limit Ad Tracking" setting. Google AdSense honours these signals where technically possible.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect names, postal addresses, phone numbers, payment details, or other personal data unless you voluntarily provide it by contacting us directly. We do not sell, rent, or trade any data we hold. We do not maintain a marketing mailing list.

Your rights under GDPR

If you are located in the UK or EU, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to the processing of any personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, email technodate3@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Irish Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Data retention

Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months as configured in Google Analytics 4. Email correspondence is retained for as long as is reasonably necessary to handle the enquiry, typically no more than 24 months.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our data processing practices or to comply with new regulatory requirements. Substantive changes will be noted at the top of this page with an updated "last modified" date.

Contact

Privacy queries, data access requests, or correction requests: technodate3@gmail.com

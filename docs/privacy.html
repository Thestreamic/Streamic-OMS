Privacy Policy

Privacy, cookies, and data transparency

This policy explains what personal data The Streamic may process, why it may be processed, how consent is handled for optional analytics and advertising, and how readers can contact us about privacy questions or correction requests.

Last updated: 27 March 2026Applies to thestreamic.inWritten for EU and India readers

Who we are

The Streamic is an independent publication focused on broadcast, media technology, streaming infrastructure, and production workflows. If you need to reach us about privacy, data requests, or corrections, use the contact details published on our Contact page.

Important: The Streamic is an editorial information site. We do not ask readers to create accounts to access ordinary content.

What data we may process

  • Server and security logs: standard technical data such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, and timestamp may be processed by hosting, CDN, or security providers.
  • Contact messages: if you email us or use a contact method we publish, we may process your name, email address, and the contents of your message in order to respond.
  • Analytics data: if you consent where required, analytics tools may measure page views, engagement, and site performance.
  • Advertising data: if advertising is enabled and consent is required in your region, ad technologies may process signals needed to serve and measure ads.
  • Local device storage: we may store a simple consent preference locally on your device so your cookie choice can be remembered.

How we use data

  • To operate, secure, and troubleshoot the site.
  • To respond to contact requests and editorial corrections.
  • To understand overall readership and improve the site, where lawful.
  • To serve advertising and measure advertising performance, where lawful and where consent is obtained if required.

Legal basis style explanation

Depending on the context, processing may be based on consent, legitimate interests in site security and operation, or the need to respond to a user communication. Where consent is required for optional analytics or advertising, we aim to ask for it before enabling those optional tools.

Cookies and similar technologies

The site uses a consent banner that defaults optional analytics and ad storage to denied until a visitor accepts. Essential technical storage may still be used for basic site operation and to remember your consent choice.

  • Essential: basic functions such as saving a consent choice.
  • Optional analytics: audience measurement and performance insights.
  • Optional advertising: ad delivery and related measurement where used.

You can reject optional cookies from the banner when shown, or clear your browser storage to reset your choice.

Advertising and third-party services

The Streamic may use third-party services such as Google Analytics and Google AdSense. Those services may process data under their own terms and privacy notices. We do not control third-party processing outside our own implementation choices.

For EEA, UK, and Switzerland traffic, optional ad and analytics storage should remain denied until consent is granted through the site’s consent flow.

International readers, EU-style transparency, and India notice content

We aim to provide a clear notice describing what categories of personal data may be processed, the purposes for processing, how to contact us, and how to raise concerns. For readers in the European Union, UK, and similar jurisdictions, this policy is intended to support transparent notice about cookies, analytics, and advertising. For readers in India, this policy is also intended to provide notice-style information consistent with the site’s limited editorial use case, including what data may be processed and why.

Retention, security, and data sharing

  • We keep contact messages only as long as reasonably necessary for correspondence, record-keeping, or legal protection.
  • Technical logs may be retained by hosting or security providers for operational and abuse-prevention purposes.
  • We do not sell a reader list. We may share data with service providers that help host, secure, analyze, or monetize the site, subject to their own legal terms.

Your choices and requests

  • You may contact us to request a correction to inaccurate personal information you have provided to us directly.
  • You may ask what contact information we hold about you if you have contacted us.
  • You may request deletion of contact communications where we no longer need to keep them.
  • You may withdraw optional cookie consent by changing your browser storage and declining optional cookies again.
Correction route: If you believe a fact on the site is materially wrong, please use the contact details on the site and include the page URL, the statement you believe is wrong, and a supporting source.

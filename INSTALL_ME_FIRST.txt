STREAMIC FINAL FIXED PACKAGE
============================

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- 10 new premium article HTML files
- cleaned high-quality live article set
- homepage section renamed to: The Streamic Intelligence
- mobile hamburger menu refined
- contact form changed from broken mailto flow to FormSubmit
- build.py and scripts/build.py updated so future rebuilds keep the fixes
- root + docs pages included for easy overwrite

HOW TO INSTALL
1. Unzip this package.
2. Open your Streamic repo folder.
3. Copy EVERYTHING from this package into your repo root.
4. Choose REPLACE / OVERWRITE ALL when asked.
5. Commit and push to GitHub.

IMPORTANT FOR CONTACT FORM
- The contact form now posts to FormSubmit using technodate3@gmail.com.
- On the first real submission, FormSubmit will send a one-time activation email.
- Open that email and confirm once.
- After that, future form messages will go directly to your inbox.

QUICK TEST AFTER DEPLOY
1. Open homepage on mobile and test hamburger menu.
2. Open Contact page and submit a test message.
3. Confirm the one-time FormSubmit activation email if it appears.
4. Open 2-3 article pages and verify images / layout.
